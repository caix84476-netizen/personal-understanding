# v2 Decision and Values Policy

Preserve how the user chooses, what they protect, what costs they accept, what costs they refuse, and whether later outcomes changed their judgment.

Values, heuristics, and decisions must all resolve back to events and verbatim captures; causes speculated by the model go into candidate hypotheses and never pose as user values.

Decision entries retain the scene, date range, options, constraints, expectations, actual outcome, reconsideration conditions, and supporting/opposing evidence. Old decisions are never deleted; new decisions only build version chains.
