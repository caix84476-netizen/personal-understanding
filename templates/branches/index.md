This file is only a routing view, never a source of truth. Authoritative records
live in `memory/records/`.

- `domain.education-career`: education, career, work, study, exams, goals
- `domain.relationships`: people, social life, trust, connection, loss
- `domain.family-home`: family, living situation, formative constraints, private space
- `domain.health-life`: health, sleep, food, stress, daily autonomy
- `domain.learning-interests`: reading, writing, hobbies, games, languages, learning habits
- `domain.self-collaboration`: working with AI, answering style, evidence, retrieval, self-understanding

Branches may overlap through record links; they are not mutually exclusive
categories. Edit these files and their keywords to match your own life.
